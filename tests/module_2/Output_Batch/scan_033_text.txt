1. TOÀN BỘ VĂN BẢN (FULL TEXT):
--------------------------------------------------
Dieu 8.Hang hoa trung gian
Trung hop hang hóa có xuat xúr tai lanh thö cua möt Nuöc thanh vien theo
quy dinh tai Dieu 7 Thong tu nay duoc sur dung de san xuat ra mot san pham khac
cac dieu kien ap dung cho san pham khac d6 khong ap dung doi vói hang hóa
düng lam nguyen liu va khong tinh dén cac nguyén lieu khong co xuat xu da
dugc sur dung trong qua trinh san xuat ra hang hóa dó.
Dieu 9.Cong gop
1. Hang h6a có xuat xú tai möt Nu&c thanh vien duqc sur dung lam nguyén
li@u cho thanh pham duqc ché bién hoac san xuat tai lanh th cua Nuc thanh vien
khac s duoc coi la nguyen lieu coxuat xur tai Nuc thanh vien noi gia cong hoac
che bién ra thanh pham.
2. Hang hóa có xuat xúr tai mt Nuöc thanh vien nhung chi trai qua cong
doan gia cöng, ché bién don gian theo quy dinh tai Dieu 11 Thong tu nay tai Nuóc
thanh vien khac thi s duqc gir nguyen xuat xur cua Nuoc thanh vien ban dau.
Dieu 10.Han mtrc linh hoat
1. Hang hóa khong dap úng quy täc chuyén doi ma sö hang hóa van duqc
coi la có xuat xur néu tri gia cua tat ca cac nguyén li@u khong co xuat xur de san
xuat ra hang hoa khong trai qua chuyén di ma sö hang hoa, khong vuot qua 20%
gia xuat xuong cua hang hoa do.
2.Tri gia cua nguyen li@u khong có xuat xü theo quy dinh tai khoan 1 Dieu
nay van durqc tinh vao V.N.M khi ap dung tieu chi Ham luong gia tri gia tang.
Diéu 11. Cong doan gia cong, ché bién don gian
1. Don gian la hoat dong khong can ky nang dac biet, may móc, dung cu
hoac thiet bi duoc san xuat hoac lap dat dac biet de thuc hien hoat dong do.Cac
mat hang thu cong do nghe nhan lam ra nhu theu, do gom, chan det may, do trang
surc thu cong va chan khau tay khong duoc coi la cong doan gia cong don gian.
2. Tron don gian la hoat döng khong can ky nang dac bit, may móc, dung
cu hoac thiet b duoc san xuat hoac lap dat dac biet de thurc hien hoat dong dó.
3. Du dap úng quy dinh tai Dieu 7 Thong tu nay, hang hoa s khong dugc
coi la c6 xuat xúr cua mt Nuc thanh vien néu cac cong doan gia cong, che bién
sau day duqc thuc hien rieng r hoac két hop vi nhau tai Nuöc thanh vien dó:
a) Giét mo dong vat.
b Cac cong doan bao quan de gir cho hang hóa van trong tinh trang tot
trong qua trinh van chuyén va luru kho nhu say kho, cap dong, thong gi6, lam lanh
va cac cong doan tuong tu.
c) Ray, rra, cat don gian, rach, uon, cuon hoac dui thang, mai sac, nghien
don gian, cat lat.
d) Lam sach, bao gom loai bo oxit, dau m, son hoac löp phu ben ngoai khac.
d) Cac cong doan son va danh bong don gian.
